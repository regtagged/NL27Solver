Monotone is four cards of one suit — 2,860 combinations, 1.06% of the deck. Only its lowest card plays, so it is never a badugi and cannot become one without drawing: every pat a monotone hand makes is a snow, which is what makes it the row to look at on the three-bet-and-pat line. There are none above a ten (a monotone ten already needs the J, Q and K of its suit), so those three rows are the four-of-a-kind hands instead — one combination each, and the solver has barely seen them. Every monotone row carries its own quads too: four aces and A♥2♥5♥9♥ show down as the same hand, and strategy here is indexed by what a hand shows down as.
